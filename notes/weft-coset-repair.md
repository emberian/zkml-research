## 1. THE RECONCILIATION — one axis, two objects, and the trade

### 1a. They are two members of one family, separated by one factor

Both start from the same proved object: the LCH novel basis `X̂ᵢ = ∏ Ŵ_b^{bit_b(i)}` of
the chain `V₀ ⊂ V₁ ⊂ … ⊂ V₅` generated by an ordered GF(2)-basis β.

| object | the map | state vector is | LCH passes |
|---|---|---|---:|
| **Weft-1** (killed) | `V₁` — evaluate on the window `V` | novel-basis coefficients | **1** |
| **coset** (weft2.md §4) | `V_{x*}` — evaluate on `x* + V` | novel-basis coefficients | **1** |
| **sysRS** (post-weft-hash.md §3) | `A = V₂·V₁⁻¹` — interpolate on `V`, evaluate on a disjoint set | **values on the window** | **2** |

> **They are the same geometric move — get the evaluation set off the interpolation
> window — applied at two different choices of what the hash state MEANS.** The coset
> form is one evaluation map. The RS form is that map precomposed with an interpolation.
> `V₁⁻¹` is the whole difference, and it is not free in either currency: it costs a
> second transform, and it drags the point set's symmetry group into the layer (§1c).

### 1b. "Zero op cost" belongs to the coset only

`[LEAN]` `cosetPack_succ`: the coset transform satisfies **the recursion of
`novelPack_succ` with exactly two changes** — the shift folds to `q_{β₀}(s)` on the way
down, and the odd branch's multiplier `X` becomes `X + s`:

```
cosetPack β s (m+1) p =
  (cosetPack β' (q_{β₀} s) m (evenPart p)).comp (foldPoly (β 0))
    + (X + C s) * (cosetPack β' (q_{β₀} s) m (oddPart p)).comp (foldPoly (β 0))
```

`[DERIVED, from the theorem]` At the *implementation* level the butterfly network is
unchanged and the shifted twiddles are precomputed constants, so the operation count is
identical — which is what `weft2.md` §4 could only assert as `[DERIVED, standard]`. The
RS form is two passes by construction: `weft2.md` §6c prices it at 160 vs 80 mults/round
and **1.68× per permutation**, and that figure stands.

### 1c. ⭐⭐ THE DECISIVE FINDING: the fast MDS form has a 2026/306-shaped flag

A lane-wise S-box preserves a subspace *for every S-box* iff the subspace is
**block-constant** — constant on the blocks of a lane partition, zero off the live blocks.
That family is exactly where 2026/306's low-weight invariant subspaces of
`M_ε = P_{t/4} ⊗ M₄` live. `weft_coset_repair.py`'s `block_constant_trail` iterates
`U_{r+1} = ` the smallest block-constant subspace containing `M·U_r`; a trail that never
reaches dimension `t` is a genuine multi-round invariant structure.

`[MEASURED]` 370 starts (all `|S| ≤ 2`, plus every regular contiguous and strided block
family), 6 rounds:

| layer | branch | trails that STALL below dim 24 | stall dimension |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Weft-1 (killed) | 6 | **370 / 370** | 21, 22, 23 … |
| **coset repair (1 pass)** | **8 EXACT** | **0 / 370** | — (full in ≤ 2 rounds) |
| sysRS, translate split (2 pass) | 25 MDS | **117 / 370** | **12** |
| sysRS, natural split 24..47 (2 pass) | 25 MDS | **117 / 370** | **12** |

Verified directly, not only by the hull iteration `[MEASURED]`: the stalled space has
blocks `(0,1)(2,3)…(22,23)` and satisfies `A·U ⊆ U` exactly. And it is the bottom of a
**3-deep flag**:

| `A = V₂V₁⁻¹`, split | `V₁`-blocks | `V₂`-blocks | `V₃`-blocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| translate `x* + V` | dim 12 **INVARIANT** | dim 6 **INVARIANT** | dim 3 **INVARIANT** |
| natural `24..47` | dim 12 **INVARIANT** | dim 6 **INVARIANT** | dim 3 **INVARIANT** |
| **coset repair** (coefficient side) | dim 12 — not invariant | dim 6 — not invariant | dim 3 — not invariant |

**The mechanism, and it is a theorem, not an instance fact.** `[DERIVED]` The
degree-`< t` polynomials invariant under translation by a GF(2)-subspace `W` are exactly
`K[Ŵ_W]`, of dimension `t/|W|`. If **both** point sets are unions of `W`-cosets, the
value vectors of that space are constant on each `W`-coset on both sides — so it is
block-constant and `A`-invariant. **A union of cosets of a common chain is precisely what
makes the LCH transform fast.** Hence:

> ⚑ **Every FAST systematic-RS split carries at least the bottom-of-chain invariant
> subspace. The escape is a point split with no common translation subspace — and that
> split has no fast transform.**

`[MEASURED]`, the escape priced by measurement: a **random** 24-point parity set gives
`deg(minpoly) = 24`, observability at all 24 lanes, and **0 stalled trails** — MDS with
none of the structure — but it is an O(t²) matmul, which deletes the entire alignment
thesis. And a *transverse* fast split (parity coset of `span{β₀+β₅, β₁..β₄}`) kills the
β-chain flag and still leaves a dim-12 block-constant invariant subspace, this time on
the `+β₁` pairing: **the structure is robust across fast splits, not an artifact of one.**

### 2a. The one-object identity is a claim, not a definition

The prize the alignment thesis was reaching for is **one proved linear object serving as
hash mixing layer AND code encoding map**. Making that explicit is easy to fake — two
structures written so that anything inhabiting one inhabits the other. It is not faked
here, and the tooth is the proof:

* `IsMixingLayer` carries a real obligation the code side does not: **no autonomous
  quotient and no invariant subspace at any level.** That obligation is in the interface
  rather than in the round schedule precisely because `autonomousSet_add_const` proves
  round constants cannot supply it.
* `IsEvaluationEncoder` **quantifies over the representing polynomial** rather than naming
  `cosetPack`, so nothing in it is true by the definition of the transform.
* ⭐ `weft_shift_zero_encodes_but_does_not_mix` shows the two are **independent**: the
  killed Weft layer is a perfectly good additive-code encoder — that was never the
  problem — and is refused the mixing obligation by the fixed lane.
* Satisfiable (`weft_one_object_satisfiable`), refutable (the tooth), not provable (the
  `s = 0` member refutes it). The floor is a floor.

### 2b. What the Lean adds over the Python, precisely

The scripts measured **one instance** — t = 24, GF(2³²), β = 2ʲ, `x* = 0xA3C17E59`. The
Lean quantifies. `novelPack_eval_zero` holds at every level and every ordered basis, where

**SURVIVES — the code side, and it is the larger half of the Lean:**

* `cosetPack` **is** the additive-code encoder on a shifted domain. Evaluating a
  BaseFold/FRI codeword on a coset rather than on the interpolation window is ordinary

  shifted twiddles, no extra operation.**
* `cosetPack_injective_of_natDegree_lt` / `_surjective_on_window` /
  `exists_unique_table_cosetPack` carry the window bijection — the property the additive
  BaseFold descent and `Selvage/AdditiveBasisBinding.lean`'s repair run on — **to the
  shifted domain**. If the additive controllers ever move to a coset evaluation domain,
  the uniqueness argument is already there.
* `novelPack_eval_zero` is a fact about the **deployed** encoder, not about a hash: the
  codeword's value at the domain point `0` is exactly the constant coefficient. ⚠ Whether
  that matters to any opening or hiding argument in the deployed additive FRI is **a
  question this lane did not assess**, stated so it is findable and not as a claim.

**DIES with the slot — the hash side:** `IsMixingLayer`, `weftRound`, everything about
autonomous quotients through an S-box, the branch numbers, and §1c's flag finding. Those
are conditional on scenario C and are banked, not deployed.
