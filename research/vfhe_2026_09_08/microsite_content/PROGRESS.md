# 8 September: teach it something new, then prove the change

[EXECUTED] A fresh sentence—“My replacement bank card still has not arrived after three weeks”—now travels through actual text encoding, encrypted teaching, a newly generated proof, a durable commit and a query against the accepted model. The complete recorded run took **42.368 seconds**. A changed proposed model root was refused; reopening recovered the accepted state. The query's decrypted score matched the integer calculation.

This demonstration started with two declared classes and only one became active. It establishes the connected path, not classification accuracy. The update has a proof; the encoder and query do not. A full reader survives. [Live teaching report](https://github.com/emberian/zkml-research/blob/dev/research/vfhe_2026_09_08/proved_journal/live_learner/REPORT.md).

[EXECUTED] A separate nonlinear learner now **squares encrypted similarities** before combining them into class scores. Its 320 ciphertext squares produced 2,560 matching individual values and 48 matching class decisions. Two new teachings changed a live query to the newly learned `travel_insurance` class. The reused-data comparison showed no improvement over the same-feature linear control, and its recipient still holds a full BFV reader. [Nonlinear learner](https://github.com/emberian/zkml-research/blob/dev/research/learn_infer_only/experiments/end_to_end/nonlinear_successor_2026_09_08/REPORT.md).

[EXECUTED] Proof coverage is reaching inside that nonlinear operation. A compiler-derived relation now checks its actual **nine-to-four modulus rescale** at 32 selected coefficient positions out of 24,576. A fresh native verifier accepted the proof and rejected a changed output digit. This covers the selected rescaling arithmetic, not the preceding multiplication or the whole learner. [Bounded rescale proof](https://github.com/emberian/zkml-research/blob/dev/research/vfhe_2026_09_08/proved_rescale_generic/REPORT.md).

[EXECUTED] A separate ring learner completed six encrypted teaching updates, two expiries and **96 matching scores** in 217.627 seconds. Sixteen recipients used independent fixed-query keys; no universal reader was constructed. These keys still reveal their registered projections on every retained input, and their coalition gets the entire sixteen-query span. This is a real narrower credential boundary, using known public data and assuming honest setup and conditional hardness. Its updates have no attached proof or history-bound release. [Restricted-key learner](https://github.com/emberian/zkml-research/blob/dev/research/learn_infer_only/experiments/end_to_end/restricted_query_learner_2026_09_08/REPORT.md).
