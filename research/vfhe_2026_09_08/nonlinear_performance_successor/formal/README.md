The final actual module checks in under five seconds without weakening its statement. Failed and interrupted attempts remain in `results/`; they are not successful checks.

[OPEN] The Rust JSON/parser, Vec execution, native range lowering/extraction, backend proof soundness, and outer ciphertext/NTT bindings remain separate obligations.
