## 2. `75%` Gated DeltaNet (`GDN`, Eqs. 1–11) — Eliminating Softmax & Unlocking Constant-State `vFHEML`

### 2.1 Ring-Generic Chunkwise WY (`C = 64`) + Flat Degree-2 Gruen `EinSumcheck` (`zkML`)
In each of the `36` GDN layers (`arXiv:2608.30320` Eqs. 1–11; `modeling_qwen4_exp.py:723-857`), `in_proj_qkv` ($2560 \to 10240$) feeds a depthwise `ShortConv` ($w_{\text{conv}}=4$, bias-free) + `SiLU`, followed by per-head `L2Norm` on $\bm{q}_t, \bm{k}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{16 \times 128}$ (with `3:1` GVA broadcast to $H_v = 48$ value heads), scalar gates $\beta_t = \sigma(\bm{W}_b \bm{x}_t) \in (0,1)^{48}$ and $\alpha_t = \exp(-\exp(\bm{A}_{\log})\mathrm{softplus}(\bm{W}_a \bm{x}_t + \bm{b}_{\text{dt}})) \in (0,1]^{48}$, and the Householder delta rule:
$$\bm{S}_t = \alpha_t(\bm{I} - \beta_t \bm{k}_t \bm{k}_t^\top)\bm{S}_{t-1} + \beta_t \bm{k}_t \bm{v}_t^\top, \qquad \bm{y}_t = \bm{S}_t^\top \bm{q}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{128} \quad (\text{per value head } h \in [48]).$$

- **Division-Free Ring-Generic WY Reduction (`[DERIVED]` `[EXECUTED]`):** Standard floating-point WY kernels (`chunk_gated_delta_rule`) compute cumulative log-decays $g_i - g_j$ and divide by $\exp(g_j)$, which fails over finite fields or when $\alpha_t \equiv 0 \pmod p$. Defining prefix products $\gamma_i = \prod_{m=0}^i \alpha_m$, suffix products $\bar{\gamma}_j = \prod_{m=j+1}^{C-1} \alpha_m$, and lower-triangular pairwise decays $\Gamma_{i,j} = \prod_{m=j+1}^i \alpha_m$ ($\Gamma_{i,i}=1$, $\Gamma_{i,j}=0$ for $i<j$) eliminates every division. On each chunk of length $C = 64$, define strictly lower-triangular $L_{i,j} = \beta_i \Gamma_{i,j} \langle \bm{k}_i, \bm{k}_j \rangle$ ($i > j$, $L_{i,j} = 0$ for $i \le j$) and solve:
  $$(\bm{I}_C + \bm{L}) \underbrace{[\bm{U} \mid \bm{W}]}_{\bm{UW} \in \mathbb{F}^{64 \times 256}} = \underbrace{[\mathrm{diag}(\bm{\beta})\bm{V} \mid \mathrm{diag}(\bm{\gamma} \odot \bm{\beta})\bm{K}]}_{\bm{RHS} \in \mathbb{F}^{64 \times 256}}.$$
  Then $\bm{U}_{\text{eff}} = \bm{U} - \bm{W}\bm{S}_{[c-1]}$, $\bm{Y}_{[c]} = (\mathrm{diag}(\bm{\gamma})\bm{Q})\bm{S}_{[c-1]} + ((\bm{Q}\bm{K}^\top) \odot \bm{\Gamma})\bm{U}_{\text{eff}}$, and $\bm{S}_{[c]} = \gamma_{C-1}\bm{S}_{[c-1]} + (\mathrm{diag}(\bar{\bm{\gamma}})\bm{K})^\top \bm{U}_{\text{eff}}$. Over BabyBear ($p = 2013265921$) across $3$ chunks ($T = 192$, forcing $\alpha_{77,2} = 0$), chunkwise WY matches sequential GDN with **`max |diff| == 0`** (`[EXECUTED]`).
- **Why One Flat Degree-2 `EinSumcheck` Is Unconditionally Sound & Complete (`[DERIVED]` `[EXECUTED]`):** Because $\bm{L}$ is strictly lower-triangular, $\bm{L}^C = \bm{0}$ over *any* commutative ring ($\det(\bm{I}+\bm{L}) = 1$, explicit inverse $\sum_{m=0}^{C-1}(-\bm{L})^m$). Thus for fixed $(\bm{L}, \bm{RHS})$, $\bm{UW}$ is **unique**: prover supplies $\bm{UW}$ directly and proves $\bm{L} \cdot \bm{UW} = \bm{RHS} - \bm{UW}$ (`hij,hjk -> hik`) via **one Gruen Mode-2 `EinSumcheck`** (`3` Gruen `{0, \infty}` rounds on $h$ + `6` quadratic rounds on $j$ = `9` rounds total; **`0` sequential GKR layers**, **`0` matrix inversions**). Mutating 1 cell of $\bm{UW}$ rejects (`Falsifier A1a`), whereas allowing $L_{0,0} = -1 \pmod p$ admits colliding witnesses $\bm{U}_1 \ne \bm{U}_2$ (`Falsifier A1b`, `[EXECUTED]`).
- **Auxiliary GDN Non-Linear Costs (`O(T)`, Zero $T^2$ Terms):** `ShortConv` is a pure linear shift ($w_{\text{conv}}=4$) + `10,240` `SiLU` lookups/tok/layer; `L2Norm` on $\bm{q}_t, \bm{k}_t$ needs **`32` `isqrt` witnesses/tok/layer** (`1,152` across 36 layers = $O(T)$, never $O(T^2)$); decay $\alpha_t$ needs **`48` scalar `EXP_TAB` lookups/tok/layer** (`1,728` across 36 layers, independent of $T$); output `RMSNormGated` uses zero-centered `(1 + weight)` + **sigmoid** gate (`output_gate_type = "sigmoid"` in `Flash-Next` vs `"swish"` in `27B`/`Max`).

### 2.2 Depth-1 Constant-State Encrypted `GDN` in `vFHEML` (`B61 + SPDZ`)
In `docs/VERDICTS.md` §3, pure-FHE softmax attention at depth $\le 3$ is refuted because $\exp$ and denominator inversion explode multiplicative depth, while growing KV ciphertexts force $O(T)$ `ct×ct` scans per token. `GDN` changes the encrypted-state asymptotics qualitatively (`[DERIVED]` `[EXECUTED]`):
1. **Constant State Size ($O(1)$ in $T$ up to `1M`):** Across all `36` GDN layers, the recurrent state holds $36 \times 48 \times 128 \times 128 = 28{,}311{,}552$ scalars (**`0.211 GiB`** in 64-bit ring shares, `3,456` `B61` ciphertexts at $N = 8192$) — **invariant whether $T = 4\text{K}, 256\text{K}$, or $1\text{M}$**.
2. **Single-Crossing Parallel-Read Identity (Homomorphic Depth 1):** Expanding $\bm{y}_t = \bm{S}_t^\top \bm{q}_t$ before writing $\bm{S}_t$ gives:
   $$\bm{m}_{k,t} = \bm{S}_{t-1}^\top \bm{k}_t, \quad \bm{m}_{q,t} = \bm{S}_{t-1}^\top \bm{q}_t, \quad \bm{u}_{\text{eff},t} = \beta_t(\bm{v}_t - \alpha_t \bm{m}_{k,t}), \quad \bm{y}_t = \alpha_t \bm{m}_{q,t} + (\bm{k}_t^\top \bm{q}_t)\bm{u}_{\text{eff},t}.$$
   Both state-vector reads ($\bm{S}_{t-1}^\top \bm{k}_t$ and $\bm{S}_{t-1}^\top \bm{q}_t$) depend solely on $\bm{S}_{t-1}$, executing **in parallel at homomorphic depth 1**, followed by **1 SPDZ crossing** (gates $\alpha_t, \beta_t$ + rank-1 outer update $\bm{k}_t \bm{u}_{\text{eff},t}^\top$). Verified bit-exact on `bfv_ref.BFV` (`61`-bit prime $t$, $3 \times 61$-bit $Q$, `[EXECUTED]`). Moreover, GDN's contractive factor $\alpha_t(\bm{I} - \beta_t \bm{k}_t \bm{k}_t^\top)$ bounds quantization/rounding noise to an $O(1)$ stationary plateau rather than $O(\sqrt{T})$ accumulation (`[SOURCE: arXiv:2609.04098 abstract]`).

## 3. `25%` Qwen Sparse Attention (`QSA`, Eqs. 12–16) — Sublinear Softmax & Top-$K_B$ Traps

Each of the `12` QSA layers (`arXiv:2608.30320` §2.1.3; `modeling_qwen4_exp.py:628-720, 860-985`) pairs core `12:1` GQA ($H_q=24, H_{kv}=2, d_h=256$, `PRoPE` on $64$ dims, zero-centered `q_norm`/`k_norm`, sigmoid output gate) with a compressed **4Q/1KV MQA ReLU Indexer**:
- **Sublinear `ReLU` Indexer (`0` `EXP_TAB` Lookups):** Raw indexer keys $\tilde{\bm{k}}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{128}$ undergo parameter-free `AvgPool` ($r = 4$) *before* `k_layernorm(128)` and `PRoPE(64)`, yielding $B = \lfloor T/4 \rfloor$ block keys $\bar{\bm{k}}_b$. Query $i$ scores strictly complete blocks ($4b + 3 \le i$, so $B_i = \lfloor (i+1)/4 \rfloor$) via:
  $$I_{i,b} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{128}} \sum_{h=1}^{4} \mathrm{ReLU}\!\left(\langle \bm{q}_i^{\text{idx},h}, \bar{\bm{k}}_b \rangle\right) \ge 0.$$
  Across a $T$-token causal prefill, `ReLU` is evaluated on $4 \sum_{i=0}^{T-1} (i/4) \approx T^2 / 2$ scalars/layer (`T` scalars/layer per decode step) using **sign-bit + byte-limb range checks and `0` `EXP_TAB` lookups** (`[DERIVED]` `[EXECUTED]`).
- **Variant (b) `Θ(B)` Threshold Top-$K_B$ (`K_B = 512`) & Two Verified Traps (`[DERIVED]` `[EXECUTED]`):** When $B_i > 512$ ($i \ge 2048$), prover commits binary indicators $\sigma_{i,b} \in \{0,1\}^{B_i}$ ($\sum_b \sigma_{i,b} = 512$) and threshold $\tau_i$ on injective tie-broken keys $\tilde{I}_{i,b} = 2^{b_{\text{idx}}} \hat{I}_{i,b} + (B_i - 1 - b) + 1$ (`0` sorting/Grand-Product columns; [moe-router-binding.md](file:///Users/ember/dev/zkml-research/notes/moe-router-binding.md#L121-L185) §2.3). Two sharp QSA-specific traps were constructed and verified in [qwen38_next_zk_vfhe_pricing.py](file:///Users/ember/dev/zkml-research/research/vfhe_sota_2026_11_21/experiments/qwen38_next_zk_vfhe_pricing.py#L709-L870):
  1. **Trap 1 (`Falsifier B1a` — 30-Bit BabyBear Bottom-$k$ Forgery):** At $T \le 256\text{K}$ ($B_i \le 65{,}536$, `16`-bit block index + `14`-bit quantized `ReLU` sum = `30`-bit key), $2 \cdot 2^{30} = 16 \cdot 2^{27} > p_{\text{BB}} = 15 \cdot 2^{27} + 1$. If `C5: RangeCheck(τ_i < 2^30)` is omitted, setting $\tau_{\text{wrap}} = 8 \cdot 2^{27} + 15$ on positive keys `[10, 20, 30, 40]` satisfies every branch range check while selecting **`{10, 20}` (bottom-$k$!) instead of `{30, 40}` (top-$k$)**! Enforcing `C5` blocks it unconditionally.
  2. **Trap 2 (`Falsifier B1b` — `ReLU == 0` Completeness Crash):** Because $\mathrm{ReLU}(\le 0) = 0$, zero-score ties at the top-$K_B$ boundary occur routinely. Without `C3` index tie-breaking, tied scores `[0, 0, 10, 20]` at $k=3$ admit **no valid threshold $\tau \in \mathbb{Z}$** ($\tau \le 0 \implies \tau - 0 - 1 < 0$ on unselected `0`; $\tau \ge 1 \implies 0 - \tau < 0$ on selected `0`), crashing honest proving!
- **2,048-to-1 Block-Fingerprint KV Gather + Bounded `K = 2048` Softmax:** Each selected block holds $r \cdot 2 \cdot H_{kv} \cdot d_h = 4 \cdot 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 256 = 4{,}096$ KV scalars (`2,048` K + `2,048` V). Binding each block via a random `Ext4` evaluation $\mathrm{eq}(\bm{r}_{\text{blk}}, \cdot)$ compresses KV gather from `2,097,152` scalar lookups to **`512` `Ext4` lookups/tok/layer**. Core softmax runs on **at most $2048 + 3 = 2051$ tokens/query** regardless of $T$, cutting per-layer decode `EXP_TAB`s by **`128×` at `256K`** and **`512×` at `1M`** (`[DERIVED]` `[EXECUTED]`).
- **Honest Amdahl Bottleneck at $T \ge 256\text{K}$ & `vFHEML` Gather:** While core softmax is $O(1)$ for $T \ge 2048$, QSA's $O(T/4)$ `ReLU + TopK` indexer grows from **`0.6%`** of decode ZK cells at `4K` (`0.12M / 18.97M`) to **`9.4%`** at `64K`, **`29.4%`** at `256K` (`7.86M / 26.71M`), and **`62.5%`** at `1M` (`31.46M / 50.31M`). Reusing QSA block indices across `next_n = 4` MTP speculative steps (`arXiv:2608.30320` §2.4, Table 4) cuts indexer share `4×` to **`9.4%` at `256K`** and **`29.4%` at `1M`** (`[EXECUTED]`). In `vFHEML`, gathering `512` of `65,536` blocks at `256K` cuts core KV `ct×ct` work `128×` if block indices open at the crossing (leaking coarse 4-token block access pattern), whereas hiding block indices requires encrypted indicator inner products (`O(T/4)` `ct×ct`, priced in §5).
