- **`StructuredExp` is strictly dominated (`0/25` grid points)** (`[EXECUTED]`, [exp_pricing_results.json](file:///Users/ember/dev/zkml-research/research/zktransformer_2026_11_20/experiments/exp_pricing_results.json#L76)): Across all 25 $(m, b)$ points and every field backend, soundness-repaired `StructuredExp` (**D3**) **never** beats `Rank1ProdExp` (**D4**) (`2.08×–5.51×` more BabyBear mul-equivalents even for unclamped steelman **D3**). - **Two additional upstream `StructuredExp` defects uncovered (`[SOURCE]`, `[EXECUTED]`)**:
  1. **`ScaleDown` mod-$p$ quotient wrap (`[SOURCE]`, [scale.rs](file:///Users/ember/.gemini/antigravity-cli/brain/7b8891ea-131f-4759-84a3-a5909e129354/scratch/zkTransformer-repo/src/basicblock/scale.rs#L41-L96))**: `ScaleDown` enforces $x - 2^{15} y + 2^{14} = \text{rem} \in [0, 2^{15})$ in $\mathbb{F}_p$ without committing or range-checking $y$; shifting $\text{rem}$ by $\delta$ lets a prover set $y' = y - \delta \cdot 2^{-15} \pmod p$, and multiplying downstream by $\text{two\_pow} = 2^{15}$ ($k=0$) cancels $2^{-15}$ back into an integer shift $-\delta$.
  2. **`ClampLower` long-context Softmax bias (`[EXECUTED]`, [builder.rs](file:///Users/ember/.gemini/antigravity-cli/brain/7b8891ea-131f-4759-84a3-a5909e129354/scratch/zkTransformer-repo/src/dag/builder.rs#L128-L130))**: Clamping inputs at $-C = -14\ln 2 \approx -9.70$ leaves a nonzero floor $2^{15-14} = 2$ ($2^{-14}$) on every tail/masked token instead of $0$, causing a **`20.14%` Softmax denominator error** (`2,344` output ULPs) at context $L = 4096$.

### 1.2 Upstream `StructuredExp` Audit: Implementation Bloat & `ScaleDown` Quotient-Wrap Bug (`[SOURCE]`, `[DERIVED]`)
Tracing [src/dag/builder.rs](file:///Users/ember/.gemini/antigravity-cli/brain/7b8891ea-131f-4759-84a3-a5909e129354/scratch/zkTransformer-repo/src/dag/builder.rs#L113-L400) and [src/basicblock/scale.rs](file:///Users/ember/.gemini/antigravity-cli/brain/7b8891ea-131f-4759-84a3-a5909e129354/scratch/zkTransformer-repo/src/basicblock/scale.rs#L17-L97) reveals two issues beyond Bugs A–D:
1. **Repo DAG bloat (`[SOURCE]`)**: `DagBuilder::exp` materializes **five** full size-$m$ dense `Role::Constant` polynomials (`mask` at L211, `c_val` at L147, `5461` at L367, `16384` at L375, `32768` at L383), executes an unconditional `mask` `Einsum` (`L124`), and runs a full degree-3 `Einsum("a,a->a")` + `ScaleDown` just to multiply $r$ by the constant scalar `5461` (`L389`). Our **D3-steelman** strips all 5 constant polynomials, folds `5461 * r` into scalar rescaling, and chooses whichever range-table granularity (`256`-entry byte table vs. `32,768`-entry 15-bit table) minimizes D3's cost at each $(m, b)$.
2. **`ScaleDown` mod-$p$ quotient-wrap vulnerability (`[DERIVED]` from [scale.rs](file:///Users/ember/.gemini/antigravity-cli/brain/7b8891ea-131f-4759-84a3-a5909e129354/scratch/zkTransformer-repo/src/basicblock/scale.rs#L45-L96) & [mod.rs](file:///Users/ember/.gemini/antigravity-cli/brain/7b8891ea-131f-4759-84a3-a5909e129354/scratch/zkTransformer-repo/src/dag/mod.rs#L624))**: In `mod.rs:624`, dense edges with `Role::Output` that have downstream consumers are **not committed**, and `ScaleDown::verify` returns `true` (`scale.rs:95`). The range check (`mod.rs:1203-1210`) checks only $x_i - 2^{15} y_i + 2^{14} = \text{rem}_i \in [0, 2^{15})$ over $\mathbb{F}_p$. For any integer shift $\delta$ keeping $\text{rem}_i + \delta \in [0, 2^{15})$, setting $y'_i = y_i - \delta \cdot 2^{-15} \pmod p$ satisfies the range equation identically over $\mathbb{F}_p$! Worse, in `exp_x = two_pow * exp_r` (`builder.rs:396`), whenever $k_i = 0$ ($\text{two\_pow}_i = 2^{15}$), multiplying $y'_i$ by $2^{15}$ cancels $2^{-15} \pmod p$ back into an exact small integer perturbation $-\delta$! Soundness requires bounding `ScaleDown` quotients as small integers; even granting **D3-steelman** free quotient bounds, it loses everywhere below.

### 2.4 Honest Refutations & Failure Boundaries of `Rank1ProdExp` (`[REFUTED: scope]`)
1. **`[REFUTED: Rank1ProdExp at (8,8) and (10,10) precision]`**: Because $T_{\text{hi}}[u_{\text{hi}}] = \mathrm{round}(2^{s_1} e^{-u_{\text{hi}}})$ steps by integer powers of $e^{-1}$ when $\text{rx}=8$, `s1 = 8` rounds $256 e^{-7} = 0.233$ to **`0`** at $u_{\text{hi}} = 7$ ($x = -7$, where Q15 still has $30$ ULPs) and incurs `57.6%` relative error on $[-6, 0]$, `8.93%` Softmax denominator error at $L=4096$, `121` ULPs on Layer-0 `o`, and `2/200` downstream argmax flips. High dynamic range requires $s_1 \ge 15$ (e.g., `(15,15)` or asymmetric `(18,12)`).
2. **`[REFUTED: Rank1ProdExp beats direct Helper-Free LogUp-GKR for m >= 2^15 at b <= 16]`**: Splitting $u = 256 u_{\text{hi}} + u_{\text{lo}}$ trades $2^b$ multiplicity cells for **`2m` lookups + `3m` committed cells** (`5m` with 1-rescale) vs. **D1b**'s `1m` lookup + `m + 2^b` cells. Once $m > m^*(b)$ (`1,912` for `gptoss-int v0`'s 12-bit support, `16,192` at $b=15$, `32,512` at $b=16$), `Rank1ProdExp` **loses** to direct **D1b** by **`1.72×–2.66×` in total muls** (`4.46×` for 1-rescale) and **`3×` in committed cells**. `Rank1ProdExp` is the right tool **only** in small-query regimes ($m \le 2^{14}$, e.g. single-layer/single-token proofs) or wide tables ($b \in \{20, 24\}$ up to $m \le 2^{18}, 2^{22}$).
3. **`[REFUTED: Zero-Rescale (15,15) single-felt Softmax accumulation in BabyBear & 3-limb INT8 GEMM]`** ([rank1_prod_exp_results.json](file:///Users/ember/dev/zkml-research/research/zktransformer_2026_11_20/experiments/rank1_prod_exp_results.json#L318-L372)): Although $E(u) \le 2^{30} < p_{\text{BB}}$, summing just **$L = 2$** tied max tokens yields $Z = 2^{31} = 2{,}147{,}483{,}648 > p_{\text{BB}}$ (wrapping mod $p_{\text{BB}}$!), and $E(0) \cdot V_j \le 2^{30} \cdot 32{,}639 \approx 2^{45} \gg p_{\text{BB}}$. Moreover, at $L \ge 512$, $|E \cdot V| \le 2^{54} > 2^{53}$ overflows `ExactF64` IEEE-754 mantissas, and on INT8 TensorCore/MXU hardware $2^{30}$ requires **`4` balanced INT8 limbs** instead of Q15's `3` limbs (`8` vs. `6` INT8 GEMMs for `E·V`, `+33%` hardware matmul cost!). Thus on **BabyBear**, `(15,15)` must either add **1 rescale** (`(15,15)->15`, costing `4.50M` vs. `2.57M` muls at $m=2^{12}$ — still `4.2×` cheaper than clamped `StructuredExp`!) or accumulate `Z` and `E·V` in 2 BabyBear limbs (whereas on **64-bit Goldilocks**, $2|\text{acc}| + Z \le 2.88 \times 10^{17} < p_{\text{GL}}/2$, fitting natively in 1 felt!).
4. **`[REFUTED: Zero-Rescale integer product in pure char-2 field arithmetic]`**: Over `GF(2^128)` (`Tower256`), native field multiplication is carryless polynomial XOR-convolution, not integer multiplication; proving $T_{\text{hi}} \cdot T_{\text{lo}}$ over $\mathbb{Z}$ requires a bit-blasted multiplier (`~225` AND bits for $15\times 15$). Hence on `Tower256`, direct 4-bit `BinaryShout` (**D2_w4**) beats **D4** across all $b \le 16, m \ge 2^{12}$.
