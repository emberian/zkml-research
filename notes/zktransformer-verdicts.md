## Executive Verdicts (Current Truth)

1. **`EinSumcheck` (`F_1 / F_{>=2} / S` multilinear contraction compiler) — ADOPT into `minidregg` (`Theory/` + `Assurance/`) and `catgrad` lowering:**
   - **What transfers (`[DERIVED]`, `[EXECUTED]`):** Partitioning free indices into $F_1$ (appearing in one operand: bound directly to verifier randomness $r_{F_1}$ with **zero** sumcheck rounds and **zero** $\widetilde{\mathrm{eq}}$ overhead) and $F_{\ge 2}$ (shared across $\ge 2$ operands: wrapped in $\widetilde{\mathrm{eq}}(r_{F_{\ge 2}}, h)$ and summed alongside contracted indices $S$) strictly generalizes our landed 2D `ZkmlMatmulSumcheck` (`F_1=\{i,k\}, F_{\ge 2}=\emptyset, S=\{j\}`) and Hadamard `cubicForm_hadamard` (`F_1=\emptyset, F_{\ge 2}=\{i\}, S=\emptyset`) into a single 6-block canonical contraction identity (`einsum_mle_identity`) reducing directly to our landed degree-3 `AirSumcheckCubic.cubicForm` (`C \equiv 0, D \equiv 1`), characteristic-free and with zero wiring commitments.
   - **Lean 4 artifact (`[EXECUTED]`):** [ZkmlEinsum.lean](file:///Users/ember/dev/zkml-research/research/zktransformer_2026_11_20/formal/Theory/ZkmlEinsum.lean) (`9.17s`) and [ZkmlEinSumcheck.lean](file:///Users/ember/dev/zkml-research/research/zktransformer_2026_11_20/formal/Assurance/ZkmlEinSumcheck.lean) (`4.97s`) compile against `/Users/ember/dev/minidregg` (`leanprover/lean4:v4.35.0`, zero `sorry`, zero custom `axiom`, zero `native_decide`, 45 `#guard_msgs`-pinned `#print axioms`, `/Users/ember/dev/minidregg` untouched; clean patch at [minidregg-einsumcheck.patch](file:///Users/ember/dev/zkml-research/research/zktransformer_2026_11_20/formal/minidregg-einsumcheck.patch)).
   - **Two high-leverage applications on `gpt-oss-20b` (`gptoss-int v0`):**
     - **Un-repeated GQA (`tgrd,ngd -> tgrn` and `tgrn,ngd -> tgrd`, `[DERIVED]`, `[EXECUTED]`):** Factoring 64 query heads as $(g, r) \in [8] \times [8]$ places intra-group query index $r$ in $F_1$ (**0 rounds**) and only KV-group $g$ in $F_{\ge 2}$ (**3 rounds**), cutting attention sumcheck rounds `12 -> 9` (and `6 + log₂ t -> 3 + log₂ t`), cutting sumcheck `Ext4` multiplications **`7.98×–8.00×`** vs `catgrad`'s `repeat_kv(8)` lowering (`gpt_oss.rs:208-219`), and avoiding `8×` KV tensor expansion (`704.64M` cells across 24 layers at $t = 4096$ if materialized).
     - **Native MXFP4 3-operand `EinSumcheck` (`ikb,jkb,jk -> ij`, `[DERIVED]`, `[EXECUTED]`):** Committing signed E2M1 integer mantissas $Q[j,k,b] \in \{0,\pm 1,\pm 2,\pm 3,\pm 4,\pm 6,\pm 8,\pm 12\}$ and per-32-element relative block scales $S[j,k] = 2^{\text{sh}[j,k]}$ directly (offline weight-domain membership enforced once at weight registration) proves $D[i,j] = \sum_{k,b} X[i,k,b]\,Q[j,k,b]\,S[j,k]$ with exact round degrees **$(2^5, 3^{\lceil \log_2 K_{\text{blk}}\rceil}, 3^{\log_2 N})$** under elimination order $b \to k \to j$ (or $(3^{\log_2 N}, 3^7, 2^5)$ under $j \to k \to b$ — **never degree 4** because no variable appears in all four factors $\widetilde{\mathrm{eq}}(r_j,j), \tilde{X}, \tilde{Q}, \tilde{S}$). Verified over BabyBear (via `jaxint.py`'s 2-limb `int8` activation split, which touches only $O(TD)$ activations) and Goldilocks (`[3,3], [3,3], [2,2,2,2,2]` in [verify_seam_and_mxfp4.py](file:///Users/ember/dev/zkml-research/research/zktransformer_2026_11_20/experiments/verify_seam_and_mxfp4.py)). Cuts `gpt-oss-20b` 24-layer MoE (`19.11B` weights) committed felts by **`1.94×`** (`1.03125` vs `2.000` `(Wh, Wl)` limbs/weight) and committed bit-volume by **`5.49–6.82×`** against the 29-bit dequantized spec window (`2.46–3.06×` like-for-like against measured ~13-bit row spread).

2. **`StructuredExp` (Cody–Waite $q = -k L + r$ + 16-entry `TwoPow` + cubic Taylor) — REJECT as import (`0/25` grid points):**
   - **Why it loses on our stack (`[DERIVED]`, `[EXECUTED]` in [exp_pricing.py](file:///Users/ember/dev/zkml-research/research/zktransformer_2026_11_20/experiments/exp_pricing.py)):**
     1. Relies on elliptic-curve `KZH-3` / `Sparse-KZH-3` over BN254 (non-PQ, trusted SRS) so zero entries in Shout one-hot matrices are free to commit; on transparent hash/code PCS (BaseFold/WHIR) or prime-field LogUp, every range/rescale sub-lookup pays per-instance witness cells.
     2. Even stripped of upstream implementation overhead (5 size-$m$ constant polynomials + extra `Einsum`) and repaired for soundness, `StructuredExp` requires $9m\text{–}17m$ committed cells per element across quotient/remainder/clamp/Taylor rescale checks, winning **`0 / 25`** points on the $(m \in \{2^{10},2^{12},2^{16},2^{20},2^{22}\}) \times (b \in \{8,12,15,16,20\})$ grid.
     3. **Comparison against `Rank1ProdExp` and direct LogUp-GKR (`[DERIVED]`, `[EXECUTED]`):**
        - Base-$2^k$ hi-lo product decomposition $\exp(x) = \exp(x_{\text{hi}})\cdot \exp(x_{\text{lo}})$ (`Rank1ProdExp`) is established prior art across `zkLLM` (§5.1), `Hao et al.` (`2025/507` §5.1), `zkAgent` (`2026/199` §3.3), and `Jolt Atlas` / `Jo` (`2026/1390` §8.5 `EXP-5`, §9 Props. 2–3 & 5). Over BabyBear, exact unrounded $T_{\text{hi}}[i]\cdot T_{\text{lo}}[j]$ has matrix rank **1** (vs rank **11** after Q15 rounding), **but** summing $\ge 2$ unrescaled products overflows BabyBear ($2 \cdot 32768^2 > p_{\text{BB}}$, and even after Q15 rescaling $2 \cdot 32768 \cdot 32639 = 2.139\times 10^9 > p_{\text{BB}}$ requires an `int8` value-limb split). With the usable **1-rescale `Rank1ProdExp`** (`5m + n_{\text{sub}}` cells), cell crossover against helper-free fraction-tree LogUp-GKR (**D1b**, `m + 2^b` cells — **`5×` fewer committed cells** than committed-inverse **D1a**) is **$m^* \approx 8,032$ at $b = 15$** ($16,192$ for 0-rescale over $\ge 64$-bit fields).
        - Consequently, on `gptoss-int v0` (`EXP_TAB` has `2,840` entries, padded to $2^{12} = 4,096$, $b = 12$) and multi-layer LLM inference ($m \ge 2^{16}$), **direct D1b helper-free LogUp-GKR** (or 4-bit `BinaryShout` **D2_w4** on `Tower256`) wins outright (`4.4×–12.8×` fewer muls than `StructuredExp`), matching Jo's Separation Principle (`2026/1390`): when table size $2^b \ll m$, decomposing addresses only multiplies per-lookup query commitments.

### 2. Architectural, Model-Layer & Paper-vs-Code Findings (`[SOURCE]`, `[DERIVED]`, `[IMPRECISE]`)

1. **Unblinded SNARK (`[SOURCE]`):** Paper Appendix D.6 (`zkTransformer.txt:1152-1162`) and `kzh3.rs:119` (`// Non-ZK KZH3 Commitment`) confirm the released implementation has zero blinding randomness: `KZH3Opening.f_star` transmits $2^{\lfloor n/3\rfloor}$ plaintext folded evaluations per opening and sumcheck rounds are unmasked.
2. **Model-layer advice & integer rescaling gaps (`[SOURCE]`, `[DERIVED]`):** `SoftmaxConst` (`basicblock/llama.rs:239`) allocates 4D `[B,H,S,S]` without row-broadcast `[B,H,S,1]` or the `sum ≈ SF` check (`dag/{gpt2,llama}.rs`); `SigmoidConst` (`basicblock/llama.rs:318-389`) has no reciprocity check; `RMSReciprocal` (`dag/llama.rs:326-374`) omits `r >= 0` and non-pow2 row masking; `ScaleDown` (`basicblock/scale.rs:45-96`) omits an integer quotient bound to prevent mod-$p$ wrap $y' \equiv y - \delta\cdot 2^{-\Delta} \pmod p$; `Role::Constant` scalars and `argmax_check` token selectors are prover-committed rather than verifier-evaluated.
3. **`ppl_repro/` vs. Rust circuit mismatch (`[SOURCE]`):** `ppl_repro/{gpt2,llama}_quant.py` evaluates `K_BITS = 5` (`31 ln 2`, matching Paper Footnote 7 for LLaMA-2), floating-point quartic Taylor, `Q30` LayerNorm/RMSNorm reciprocal advice, and cubic tanh-GELU (`gpt2_quant.py:80-198`), whereas the benchmarked Rust circuit (`exp.rs:16`, `builder.rs:131, 421`, `dag/gpt2.rs:63-76`, `config.yaml`) runs `K_BITS = 4` (`14 ln 2`, leaving a `2`-ULP Q15 floor at `s = 15` across unmasked causal tails), cubic Taylor, and `1.702x` QuickGELU. In addition, `Einsum::prove` (`einsum.rs:552`) invokes equal-arity `LinearSumcheckProver`, so unequal-residual contractions like Appendix F Eq. (9) `bsdhv,btdh->bsth` (`dim(v) > 1`) are not yet projected in `src/`.
4. **Paper Lemma 3.2 (`[IMPRECISE: Lemma 3.2 round parameter v vs ρ; holds conservatively for all shipped Einsums]`):** Conditioned on an incoming evaluation claim $\tilde{Z}(r) = c$ (with outer $|F|/|\mathbb{F}|$ charged once at the DAG root), Lemma 3.2 writes $(m+1)v/|\mathbb{F}|$ with $v = \max_t |\mathcal{I}_t|$ instead of the exact sumcheck round count $\rho = \sum_{u \in S \cup F_{\ge 2}} \log_2 D_u$. Whenever one operand carries all summed indices (true for every equation in `src/dag/*.rs`), $\rho \le v$; when operands carry disjoint summed indices (e.g. `bp,bq->b` with $2^1, 2^2, 2^2$, proved in `ZkmlEinSumcheck.lean`), $v = 3 < \rho = 5$.
