# One durable restricted-ring semantic run

[PREDECLARED] Use the completed linear semantic fixture and its byte-identical
general-B registry/transport. Create fresh full-profile A, sixteen honest
recipient-local keys/registrations, and 561 directly uniform missing public
rows. No old key/state, public seed, universal reader or missing-row secret is
an input. The parameter set and exact Gaussian/cutoff implementation are those
already fixed by this backend; no cryptographic hardness claim is measured.

[PREDECLARED] Interleave six fresh encodings/teachings across `card_arrival` and
`cash_withdrawal_charge`, capacity two, using the fixed three cached training
observations per class. Events five and six expire the actual original inputs
one and two. Every candidate is fully recomputed by a separate public actor as
`acc + fresh - old` in this ring, including all ring/mask coefficients and the
canonical context/lineage header, and compared byte-for-byte before commit.
This is deterministic complete verification, not a succinct/compiler proof.

[PREDECLARED] Before accepting event five, change one canonical ring
coefficient of a candidate copy, update its declared file/model hashes, and
require full recomputation to refuse it with no head change. A distinct stale
parent request must also refuse. Reopen the journal in a fresh public process
at revision four, continue through both expiries, then publish the exact
accepted revision-six model. Exact retry may return its retained receipt.

[PREDECLARED] After public operations, source checks, public publication and
actor shutdown, sixteen separate recipient processes read their registered
queries on the two final accepted class states: 32 scores and 16 decisions.
Compare these deliberately public known-fixture results with the frozen
precomputed reference. Each recipient wrapper checks genesis, current accepted
head/model, registry, its registration/coordinate and exact class hashes before
calling the unchanged decoder. The public actor is denied all recipient private
paths; each recipient is denied other recipients' private paths and networking.

[SCOPE] The service supplies durable continuity/integrity and accepted-state
visibility. The keys are fixed query projections, cryptographically narrower
than a universal BFV reader under the scheme's assumptions, but remain usable
outside the journal on retained inputs or allowed combinations. A coalition
gets its per-input registered-query span. This is not cryptographic
history-bound release, full resident privacy, natural-language ambiguity or a
fresh accuracy benchmark. Encoding/FIFO/authorization, honest setup, samplers,
Python/FLINT/codec, filesystem and shared-OS administration remain assumptions.

[PREDECLARED] One normal joined run, at most fifteen minutes overall and
180 seconds per actor. No model/encoder rerun, tuning, seed search, extraction
or unrelated routing tests. Frozen predecessor/companion bytes are preserved;
root owns shared ledgers and commits.
