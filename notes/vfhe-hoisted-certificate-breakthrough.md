## 2. `HoistedB61`: Four Stacked `vFHE` Certificate Breakthroughs

### 2.1 `(a)` Hoisted Sum-Before-Rescale (`Lazy Rescale`: `6,144 -> 128` Matvec Certificates)
At $N = 8192, d = 4096$, `fhe_layout` packs $d_r = 128 \times d_c = 64$ entries per ciphertext (`32 × 64 = 2,048` blocks/layer) `[SOURCE: step_counts.py:20-23,113-117]`. Baseline `step_counts.py` rescaled all `2,048` blocks per matvec (`6,144` across `z1 = W1 x`, `z2 = W2 h`, `b = W2^T d2`) *before* summing across the contracted block index (`step_counts.py:155-167`).

In `HoistedB61`, for each output group $r$ (summing $K = 64$ col-blocks on forward `z1, z2` = `32 + 32` groups, and $K = 32$ row-blocks on transpose `b = W2^T d2` = `64` groups, reusing the **exact same** `cW2` ciphertexts without transpose key-switching), the prover accumulates the unrescaled integer tensor over $\mathbb{Z}[X]/(X^N+1)$ first and rescales **once** (`[DERIVED]`):
$$D_k^{(r)}(X) = \sum_{c=0}^{K-1} \text{pair}_k\bigl(A^{(r,c)}(X), B^{(c)}(X)\bigr) \bmod (X^N+1), \qquad y_{k,j}^{(r)} = \left\lfloor \frac{t \cdot D_{k,j}^{(r)} + \lfloor Q/2 \rfloor}{Q} \right\rfloor.$$
- **Fits in existing $k = 5$ `61`-bit extension primes $B_p$ (`[DERIVED]`, `[EXECUTED]`):** With $\log_2 Q \approx 183$, $\log_2 t \approx 61$, $N = 8192$, $K = 64$:
  $$|D_{1,j}^{(r)}| \le K \cdot 2 N (Q/2)^2 < 2^{384}, \quad |y_{1,j}^{(r)}| < 2^{262}, \quad |t D_{1,j}^{(r)} + \lfloor Q/2\rfloor| + |Q y_{1,j}^{(r)} + s_{1,j}^{(r)}| < 2^{446} \ll Q \cdot B \approx 2^{488},$$
  leaving **`42` bits of spare CRT no-wrap margin** (`36` bits spare even at $K = 2,374$ for `V = 151,936` LLM PIR!).
- **Strictly smaller decryption noise + Lean 4 mechanization (`[DERIVED]`, `[EXECUTED]`):** Eager per-block rescaling accumulates $K$ independent $[-1/2, 1/2]$ rounding errors; hoisting pays **one** rounding error (`+1` vs `+K`). On real `bfv_ref` ciphertexts at $K_{\text{blk}}=64$, zero-error rounding noise drops **`589 -> 77` (`7.65×` smaller)** `[EXECUTED: vfhe_hoisted_b61_and_pir.py:1158-1182]`. Companion Lean module [`BfvHoistedRescale.lean`](../research/vfhe_sota_2026_11_21/formal/Theory/BfvHoistedRescale.lean) kernel-proves exact phase decomposition `sumMulPhase_encrypt_eq` (`L120`), noise bound `sumMulPhase_noise_le` (`L156`), deployed $K=64$ margin `deployed_hoisted64_margin_holds` (`L274`), and `eager_vs_hoisted_rounding_gap` (`L299`, where $K=4$ eager roundings mis-decrypt `0` as `1` while hoisted decrypts to `0`).
