## Full registry, domain and concrete hash recipe

[SOURCE: construction] The driver creates one fresh authentication registry, then invokes the inherited honest recipient initialization separately for all 16 ordered rows before its sole public-build call: adapter `driver.py:118–127`. The inherited `setup.py:92–114,123–138` fixes the registry hash, row identities and row hashes; validates signatures against each registered Ed25519 verification key; and samples each dedicated scalar independently. New `seed_setup.py:17–30` checks every announcement and public A subgroup and includes the complete ordered registry, A values, rows, group parameters, dimension, capacity, pivot rule, suite and cap in the domain. This review adopts the contract's honest fresh registration premise; it does not certify an adversarial registration service or OS randomness.

[SOURCE: implementation] `derive.py:15–28,43–70` hashes the full canonical domain bytes, fixed seed, role, coordinate and counter with explicit length framing. The domain digest is inventory only. The frozen wrapper builds integer/string/list data and the inherited registry parser rejects floats and duplicate keys. The small canonical helper itself is not a general no-float schema validator; this acceptance covers the fixed wrapper-produced domain. The named primitive is `hashlib.shake_256`, not a public sampled scalar exponentiation. Each coordinate accepts the first high-bit candidate in `[0,q)` or `[1,p)`, stops at its first acceptance and otherwise rejects after exactly 128 attempts. Raw blocks retain unused suffix bits. Tau zero and U equal to either sign of one are accepted; U zero is rejected.

[EXECUTED helper checks] [helper_review.py](helper_review.py) independently checks seven framing cases, 18 domain/role/coordinate/counter inputs, all 512 public one-byte examples for two high-bit widths, and two production-width extraction examples. These controls do not run production seeds or search for favorable seeds. Their retained results are in [helper_results.json](helper_results.json). An independent exact-rational elimination of the public 16-column pivot gives determinant `-812032080`; no group backend was used.
